# Map asset notice

`india-outline.svg` is a schematic navigation background generated from the country/coastline boundary data distributed with Basemap Data 2.0.0. Basemap Data is distributed under LGPL-3.0-or-later. The SVG is used only as a geographic orientation layer.

State/UT corpus markers are placed from approximate centroid coordinates maintained in `analysis/scripts/build_public_atlas.py`. They are not incident coordinates, legal boundary claims, or prevalence estimates.
