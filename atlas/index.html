METHOD / INTERPRETATION

How to read the Atlas

The interface is a public exploration layer over a versioned research dataset. The research repository remains the source of truth.

01

What counts as a case?

The Atlas reconstructs publicly documented, India-connected incidents in which social engineering materially enabled cybercrime or digitally mediated fraud. Inclusion and exclusion are governed by the repository methodology, not by whether an incident is famous or easy to find.

02

Sampling boundary

The corpus is purposively sampled and retrieval-driven. Public-record availability, searchability, procedural stage and deliberate diversity retrieval affect what enters the Atlas. Counts are not national prevalence, state rankings, average-loss estimates or demographic risk estimates.

03

Source hierarchy

T1 covers judicial and formal adjudicatory material. T2 covers official institutional material. T3 covers credible journalism with substantive case detail. Procedural posture is preserved because a bail order, consumer appeal and final criminal judgment answer different questions.

04

Incident and actor layers

cases.csv asks what happened. actors.csv asks what a source can connect to a particular actor-role and conduct. One actor row is not necessarily one person, and it is not a finding of guilt.

05

Human identity resolution

Identified and partially identified actor records resolve to a real-world human under the Atlas codebook. A persona, alias, bank account, SIM, device, IP address or broad conspiracy allegation does not by itself resolve a human identity.

06

Attribution scale

Strong
Multiple independent streams or an adjudicated finding support the relevant conduct.
Moderate
Meaningful linkage exists but a material inferential step or plausible alternative remains.
Limited
Association is established but the relevant conduct is not substantially established.
Unclear
The public material is too incomplete or ambiguous.
Not assessed
There is insufficient material to evaluate the conduct.
07

Reconstruct broadly. Attribute conservatively.

The Atlas deliberately distinguishes endpoints from humans and association from conduct. A receiving account can be highly relevant evidence without proving that its holder operated the victim-facing deception. Every case page therefore exposes both what the record supports and the primary evidentiary gap.

08

Reproducibility

The public JSON is generated from the authoritative CSVs. It is not edited separately. The repository contains data dictionaries, controlled vocabularies, codebooks, screening history, source registry, case narratives and validators.
