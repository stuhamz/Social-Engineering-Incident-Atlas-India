INTERPRETATION BOUNDARY

Corpus counts are not prevalence estimates.

The Atlas is assembled through documented, purposive public-record retrieval. Court stage, source availability and searchability influence what enters the corpus and what becomes visible inside each record.

Use this interface to explore the reviewed material, compare evidence and inspect coding. Do not interpret state, category or source-stage counts as a representative estimate of Indian cybercrime.
