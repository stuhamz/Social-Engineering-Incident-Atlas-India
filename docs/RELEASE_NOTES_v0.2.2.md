# Release notes v0.2.2

Wave 1A is the first post-re-audit expansion of the Social Engineering Incident Atlas India.

The release adds 10 reviewed incidents, moving the active corpus from 74 to 84 cases, and adds 24 actor-role assessments and 11 T1 sources. The v0.2.0 schema is unchanged.

Five newly represented jurisdictions are added in this batch: Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Jammu and Kashmir, Puducherry, and Andaman and Nicobar Islands.

The batch also extends temporal coverage back to 2008 and adds evidence-rich appellate material involving phishing infrastructure, CDRs, subscriber records, Section 65-B certificates, CERT-In analysis and device-forensics gaps.

`SEIAI-0060` remains retired and no stable ID is recycled or renumbered.
