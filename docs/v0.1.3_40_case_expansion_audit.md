## Audit decisions

1. **No schema break.** The v0.1.2 actor-role model remains sufficient for the ten new cases.
2. **Attempted incidents are permitted when the social-engineering sequence and requested action are well documented.** SEIAI-0038 therefore records a BEC attempt with blank `financial_loss_inr`.
3. **Foreign-currency losses are not researcher-converted.** SEIAI-0039 records the USD amount narratively but leaves `financial_loss_inr` blank.
4. **Civil/consumer findings are not criminal guilt findings.** Those sources can establish transaction/authentication facts or contractual liability while offender attribution remains `unclear` or `not_assessed`.
5. **Category-diversity stopping rule retained.** Several otherwise eligible investment/digital-arrest records remain pending rather than further saturating already common categories.
6. **No source-claim table yet.** The actor layer still resolves the most important attribution-compression issue without requiring proposition-level annotation.

Both `validate_dataset.py` and `validate_actors.py` must report zero errors and zero warnings before publication.
