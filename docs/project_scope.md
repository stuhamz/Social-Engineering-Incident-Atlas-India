# Project Scope

The Social Engineering Incident Atlas India studies publicly documented incidents in India where human manipulation is materially relevant to the offence.

It is not a general cybercrime database and it is not a prevalence survey.

The unit of analysis is the **incident**. A single incident may have multiple sources, multiple accused or unidentified participants, multiple victim-facing or financial functions, and multiple evidence types.

The Atlas separates incident-level reconstruction from actor-level attribution.

The project records attack structure, communication channels, target actions, psychological mechanisms, financial consequences where source-supported, evidence types, procedural posture, actor functions, identity resolution, conduct-specific attribution strength, and evidentiary limitations.

The corpus is purposively assembled from public sources. It should be used to study documented patterns and evidentiary structure within the reviewed corpus, not to estimate national or state prevalence.
