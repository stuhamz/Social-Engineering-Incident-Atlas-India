# v0.2.2 Wave 1A expansion audit

## Scope

v0.2.2 expands the corrected v0.2.1 corpus from **74 to 84 active reviewed incidents** while retaining the v0.2.0 incident/actor schema and the retired `SEIAI-0060` history.

Wave 1A was not a prevalence-oriented expansion. It was selected to improve:

- geographic coverage
- temporal depth
- final/appellate and procedural-stage diversity
- non-WhatsApp social-engineering mechanisms
- digital-evidence richness
- separation between victim-facing and financial/organisational attribution

## Methodological notes

The batch deliberately includes several cases where public records reveal endpoints more strongly than the original social-engineering operator. Particular stress tests include:

- a 2008 phishing case where a missing GEQD phone/SIM report caused an appellate remand
- a 2012 hacked-email transfer case where the manipulated human layer is bank staff
- a PMO impersonation case where the consequential action is provision of government facilities rather than direct financial loss
- a Port Blair digital-arrest case where the prosecution's `mastermind` allegation was supported primarily by a co-accused statement
- a Delhi digital-arrest case with a well-resolved financial endpoint but unresolved victim-facing caller identities

## Validator change

`validate_full_audit.py` is made count-dynamic in v0.2.2. It no longer hard-codes a specific corpus size. It continues to enforce:

- one narrative per active case
- no retired-ID resurrection
- unique case/actor/source IDs
- no cross-case duplicate source URLs
- source ownership
- actor-source ownership
- date-format/ordering checks
- at least one actor and source per active case
- transparent preservation of the `CAND-0079` duplicate history

This change is necessary for safe future expansion toward 200 cases.
