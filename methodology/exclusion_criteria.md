# Exclusion Criteria

Exclude a candidate when any of the following applies.

## Purely technical compromise

Exclude incidents in which the available account describes only exploitation, malware, credential theft, or unauthorised access without meaningful human manipulation.

## Insufficient detail

Exclude reports that merely state that a person was "cyber defrauded" or "cheated online" without enough detail to reconstruct the social-engineering component.

## Source quality insufficient

Exclude incidents supported only by unverifiable reposts, anonymous social media claims, promotional content, or sources that cannot be independently accessed or checked.

## Duplicate incident

Multiple sources concerning the same underlying incident are not separate cases. Link them to one canonical `case_id`.

## India not materially involved

Exclude incidents with only incidental mention of India.

## Outside scope

Exclude ordinary offline fraud without a meaningful digital or cyber component, unless the project scope is formally expanded in a later version.

## Ambiguous cases

When uncertain, record the candidate as `pending` in the screening log rather than forcing inclusion.
