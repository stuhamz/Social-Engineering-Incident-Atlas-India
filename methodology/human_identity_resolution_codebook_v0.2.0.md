# Human Identity Resolution Codebook v0.2.0

This codebook governs the Atlas `identity_resolution` field.

- **identified**: specific real-world human resolved with actor-specific source linkage.
- **partially_identified**: source points to a specific real-world human, but the role linkage is materially incomplete, indirect, contested or non-final.
- **actor_cluster**: a defined group/numbered accused cluster is visible, but no specific real-world human is resolved for the assessed conduct.
- **unknown**: the public record does not resolve a specific real-world human.

## Exclusion rules

The following do not, by themselves, resolve a human:

- alias, persona or first name;
- profile/account display name;
- bank-account holder association;
- SIM subscriber association;
- device/IP/platform endpoint association;
- broad conspiracy allegation;
- unnamed or numbered accused.

## Important distinction

Identity resolution is not a finding of guilt. It measures what the public source makes visible about a human actor linked to a specified function or conduct.
