# Inclusion Criteria

A record is included only when all mandatory conditions are met.

## Mandatory conditions

### 1. India is materially involved

At least one of the following must apply:

- the victim or principal target is in India
- the investigated conduct materially occurred in India
- the relevant criminal investigation or court proceeding is in India
- the financial or communications trail materially involves India

### 2. Human manipulation is causally relevant

The incident must contain a documented or strongly source-supported attempt to influence a human decision through deception, impersonation, pretext, authority, urgency, fear, trust, relationship-building, information elicitation, or a comparable social mechanism.

The manipulation does not have to be the only attack mechanism.

### 3. A consequential action or attempted action exists

Examples include:

- transfer of funds
- disclosure of credentials, OTPs, personal data, or confidential information
- installation of software
- approval of a payment or transaction
- account access
- continuation of communication under a false pretext
- other action materially advancing the offence

Attempted cases may be included when the source contains enough detail to reconstruct the technique and evidence.

### 4. Sufficient public detail exists

The available sources must permit coding of at least:

- the broad contact or social-engineering mechanism
- the principal requested or induced action
- the incident context
- at least one meaningful investigative, evidentiary, or attribution feature

### 5. Source threshold is met

At least one usable source must be available from the accepted source hierarchy in `source_quality.md`.

## Inclusion does not imply guilt

A case may be included at investigation, bail, trial, or appeal stage. The dataset must preserve the procedural posture and avoid converting allegations into findings.
