## Neutral case summary

A fake Facebook profile used film producer Rockline Venkatesh's identity to send friend requests and allegedly offered a woman a film opportunity while soliciting sexual compliance and personal details. The final criminal trial ended in acquittal after the complainant turned hostile, acknowledged a compromise and did not support the prosecution. The court expressly stated that the identity of the accused as the profile operator had not been established. The Atlas therefore separates the visible deceptive persona from unresolved human attribution.
