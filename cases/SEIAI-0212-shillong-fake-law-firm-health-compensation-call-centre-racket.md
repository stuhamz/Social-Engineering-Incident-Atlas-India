## Neutral case summary

In August 2017, police investigating call-centre fraud in Shillong identified Dasanbha Enterprise as a separate alleged racket targeting American citizens. The operation allegedly raised health concerns and then represented that a top law firm could secure compensation, extracting dollar payments from victims. The petitioner was alleged to own and run the call centre.
