## Neutral case summary

In August 2017, Meghalaya police uncovered a Shillong call-centre operation alleged to be impersonating US Internal Revenue Service agents. According to the FIR material reproduced in the bail order, American citizens were told they had tax defaults and threatened with punitive action unless they paid. The petitioner was alleged to have worked as a mobilizer recruiting people for the operation.
