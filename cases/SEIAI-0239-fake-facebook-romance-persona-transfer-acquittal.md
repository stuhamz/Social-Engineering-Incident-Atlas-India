## Neutral case summary
The prosecution alleged that a fake Facebook profile called Syedaliya.mujasim used false photographs, befriended Mohammed Waseem Ahamed and cultivated a romantic relationship before requesting money for educational and other expenses. The complainant said he transferred INR 1,400,000 to several accounts and was later threatened when he questioned the scheme. In the final trial of accused No.2 Harshiya Khanam, the court found the documentary evidence did not corroborate the claimed transfer and communication links: no relevant CDR connected her to the calls, Facebook records had not been investigated, bank extracts did not show the alleged deposits to her account, and CCTV evidence was not proved in accordance with law. She was acquitted.
