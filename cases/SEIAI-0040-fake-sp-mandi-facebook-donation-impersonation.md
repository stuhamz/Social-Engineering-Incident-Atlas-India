## Procedural posture

- Public status coded: `trial`
- Disposition: Regular bail granted on 17 December 2025 after investigation and charge sheet; trial was underway and observations were expressly non-merits.

## Neutral case summary

A Facebook profile impersonating SP Mandi solicited donations for a fictitious/unsupported medical-emergency narrative. Facebook, Google, IP/TSP and bank records were used to connect the profile and payment trail to subscriber/account information associated with investigated persons. One donor was documented as transferring INR 10,501, with additional small donations reported.

## Reconstruction

### Target

- Target type: `other`
- Context: members of the public / potential donors
- Target selection: `no`

### Reconnaissance

- Coded: `yes`
- Detail: The fake profile copied the real SP Mandi identity and image. Provider records later tied access of the fake profile/Gmail to subscriber-linked mobile numbers.

### Initial contact

A fake Facebook account was created in the name/profile of the Superintendent of Police, Mandi and used to send friend requests/messages to members of the public.

### Pretext

The account posted an image/story of a child purportedly suffering from liver cancer whose father had died and solicited donations while appearing to speak on behalf of SP Mandi.

### Requested action

Donate money through the payment/account details presented through the fake Facebook profile.

### Victim action / consequence

Members of the public made small donations; one documented donor transferred INR 10,501 in two payments and other smaller donations were also reported.

## Attribution analysis

### Incident-level attribution target

Applicant’s alleged operation of the fake SP Mandi Facebook profile, assessed separately from control of the receiving bank account.

### Attribution strength

**moderate**

### Limitations

Provider/IP/subscriber and investigation material create substantial prima facie linkage, but the source is a bail order and the applicant’s reported disclosure is not treated as a final finding of guilt.

### Alternative explanation

Subscriber/IP association should be corroborated with device/session evidence; the receiving account was reported under a similar but not identical name and should not be collapsed into the profile-operator identity without further proof.

### Primary evidentiary gap

Full device/account-session attribution and beneficiary-account control evidence tying the profile creation, messages and withdrawal of donations to the same human operator.

## Actor-role decomposition

- **Fake SP Mandi Facebook profile operator** — `moderate` for: Creating/operating a Facebook profile impersonating SP Mandi and using a child-cancer donation story to solicit money. Limitation: Facebook/Google/IP/TSP/subscriber evidence and investigation material provide substantial prima facie linkage, but this is a bail-stage source and reported disclosures are not final findings.
- **Donation receiving/withdrawal role** — `limited` for: Receiving or withdrawing donations generated by the fake SP profile. Limitation: The source names a receiving account under a similar but not identical name and reports that the applicant withdrew/spent donations; full KYC/control/device corroboration for the financial account is not reproduced in the order.
