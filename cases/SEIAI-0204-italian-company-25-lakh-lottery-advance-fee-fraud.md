## Neutral case summary

A Bihar informant received a phone call stating that she had won an INR 25 lakh lottery from an Italian company. The caller demanded an initial INR 15,000 and then further charges framed as tax and other requirements. The informant ultimately paid INR 140,000 through bank deposits.
