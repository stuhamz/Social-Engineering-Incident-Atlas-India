## Neutral case summary

Shyamal Dutta created and promoted BEGC as if it were a Government of India body, using official-looking nomenclature, seals, Gazette publications, websites, bank arrangements and a payment gateway. Job seekers applied for advertised posts and paid fees because they believed the organisation was governmental. The final CBI trial judgment convicted Dutta for cheating, forgery-related offences and improper use of the State Emblem, but acquitted him on conspiracy and IT Act 66D.
