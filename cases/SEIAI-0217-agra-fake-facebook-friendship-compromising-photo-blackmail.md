## Neutral case summary

An Agra woman said she was befriended on Facebook in 2016 by a man using a false identity and personal backstory. After building trust, he allegedly obtained INR 5 lakh and substantial gold jewellery. When repayment was sought, she said she was lured to a room, incapacitated, photographed in a compromising state and then blackmailed for recurring payments. The same report described several other women making similar allegations against the same man. The Atlas isolates this Sadar-area victim as the focal incident and does not aggregate the other women's losses into this record.
