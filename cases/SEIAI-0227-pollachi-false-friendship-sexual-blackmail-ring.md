## Neutral case summary

The Pollachi case involved an organised group that lured women through false friendships, moved the interaction into private settings, sexually abused victims, recorded the abuse and used the resulting material for blackmail. In May 2025, after six years of proceedings, a Mahila Special Court in Coimbatore convicted all nine defendants and imposed life sentences. Verdict reporting says eight survivors testified and the court considered extensive documentary and digital evidence. The Atlas records the social-engineering and blackmail mechanism at the incident-network level while avoiding the unsupported assumption that every convicted defendant performed every stage against every survivor.
