## Neutral case summary

A Mumbai job seeker received a Facebook message in September 2017 offering a FlyDubai cabin-crew position. The scheme used purported recruiter communications, forged tickets and selection or appointment letters. The complainant paid INR 1,076,000 in total before discovering the job was not genuine.
