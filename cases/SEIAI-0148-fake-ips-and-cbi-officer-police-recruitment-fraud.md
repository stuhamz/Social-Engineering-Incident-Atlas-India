## Neutral case summary

Three Punjab victims allegedly paid INR 3.15 million after petitioner Anmol Rattan promised police appointments and presented himself as an IPS officer, with his wife portrayed as a CBI ASP. Social-media, WhatsApp and alleged identity material reinforced the authority pretext. The petitioner disputed the allegations, and the High Court treated the evidence only prima facie while denying anticipatory bail.
