## Neutral case summary

A Kerala complainant followed an ASK-IATOP advertisement and was contacted on WhatsApp by a persona calling herself Riya Rawat. He was added to '484-ASK-HNW Capital', promised investment profit-sharing and transferred INR 32.409 million. The victim-facing persona remains unresolved.

## Reconstruction

### Initial contact

After seeing an online advertisement for ASK-IATOP, the complainant clicked a link and received a WhatsApp message from a person using the name Riya Rawat.

### Pretext

The persona added the complainant to a WhatsApp group named '484-ASK-HNW Capital' and promised profit-sharing on investment amounts through the purported ASK-IATOP operation.

### Requested action

Invest through the purported ASK-IATOP/HNW Capital scheme by transferring funds as directed.

### Victim action and consequence

From 18 March 2025 the complainant transferred a total of INR 32,409,000 on different days under the investment-profit pretext.

- Reported financial loss: INR 32409000
- Payment method: `bank_transfer`
