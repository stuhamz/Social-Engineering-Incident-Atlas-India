## Neutral case summary

A retired professor in Visakhapatnam was recruited into a WhatsApp group styled as H-10 Nuvama Health Group and directed to a fraudulent investment link borrowing the identity of a known financial brand. A small successful withdrawal built trust before much larger transfers and later withdrawal commissions were demanded. The court record describes approximately INR 20 million in cheating. The petitioner's alleged connection depended on disputed data from a second-hand phone.
