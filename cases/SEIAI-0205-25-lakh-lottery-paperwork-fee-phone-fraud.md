## Neutral case summary

In July 2014, an Uttar Pradesh informant received a phone call announcing an INR 25 lakh lottery. She was told that paperwork and formalities required advance payments and deposited INR 61,000 across five bank accounts before realizing the lottery was fictitious. A later quashing application was dismissed, leaving the criminal proceedings to continue.
