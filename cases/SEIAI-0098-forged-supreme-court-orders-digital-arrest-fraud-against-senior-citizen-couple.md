## Neutral case summary

A senior-citizen couple reported a digital-arrest scam running from 3 to 16 September 2025 in which callers posed as CBI, Enforcement Directorate and judicial authorities. They displayed forged Supreme Court orders through WhatsApp/video conferencing and threatened arrest and property seizure. The couple transferred INR 10.55 million. The Supreme Court treated the forged judicial documents as sufficiently serious to initiate suo motu proceedings, but the public order does not identify the fraud operators.

## Reconstruction

### Initial contact

A senior-citizen couple reported being contacted between 3 and 16 September 2025 by persons impersonating CBI, Enforcement Directorate and judicial authorities through telephone and video calls.

### Pretext

The operators displayed forged Supreme Court orders and other purported freeze, arrest, surveillance and court documents, claimed criminal investigation, and threatened arrest and property seizure under a digital-arrest pretext.

### Requested action

Remain compliant with the purported authorities and transfer funds through multiple bank transactions under the claimed digital-arrest/investigation process.

### Victim action and consequence

The couple transferred INR 10,550,000 through multiple bank transactions.
