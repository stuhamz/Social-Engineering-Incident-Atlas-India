## Neutral case summary

Niranjan Chidananda Bhat was prosecuted for creating a fake email identity in the name of Dr A.P.J. Abdul Kalam and using it to recommend himself to the Hoover Medal Board in New York. The record included the original email identity, computer evidence, hard disks, a mobile phone and extensive documentary material. The conviction survived trial, first appeal and High Court revision.
