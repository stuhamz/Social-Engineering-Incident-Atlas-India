## Neutral case summary

In 2022, Jodhpur victim Arvind Kalani was induced by unknown cyber-fraud operators to participate in a guaranteed-profit investment-cum-MLM scheme. He and his brother transferred INR 162,621,387 through 101 transactions into eight bank accounts. A later PMLA order reconstructs extensive layering of those proceeds and preserves the distinction between the original victim-facing fraud and downstream laundering actors.
