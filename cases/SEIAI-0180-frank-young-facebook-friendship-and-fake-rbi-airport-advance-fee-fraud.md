## Neutral case summary

An Odisha victim befriended a Facebook persona called Frank Young and was told that valuable foreign gifts were being sent to India. A layered set of supporting callers and emails posing as security, RBI and airport officials then demanded fees for release, remittance and foreign exchange. The victim paid INR 1,703,390 into four accounts. The case contains CDR, bank and seized-device evidence linking an alleged Nigerian participant to the wider operation.
