## Neutral case summary

Two Indian soldiers posted at Pokhran in Rajasthan were reported to have been honey-trapped by a woman they met on Facebook. Investigators said the relationship continued for roughly a year and a half and that strategic information was sent through Facebook, WhatsApp and related channels to a Pakistani intelligence-linked persona. The soldiers were intercepted at Jodhpur railway station in November 2019; one was reported arrested and the other as cooperating with investigators. The Atlas records the unresolved female persona/handler as the social-engineering actor and keeps the soldiers' alleged disclosure conduct analytically separate.
