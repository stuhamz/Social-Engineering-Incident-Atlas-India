## Neutral case summary

A Wayanad complainant was offered a Telegram part-time job, directed to a website called yumdishes.stores and promised commissions for food-rating reviews purportedly for YUM Brands. The complainant transferred INR 3.3 million. The bail court expressly found the public material weak as to the particular applicant, so actor attribution remains unresolved.

## Reconstruction

### Initial contact

The complainant was offered a part-time job through Telegram and directed to register on www.yumdishes.stores.

### Pretext

The website and Telegram operators promised substantial commission for rating/reviewing food items purportedly for YUM Brands Pvt. Ltd.

### Requested action

Register on the website, perform rating/review tasks and transfer funds as required by the scheme.

### Victim action and consequence

Between 7 February and 1 March 2024 the complainant transferred a total of INR 3,300,000 under the purported part-time job scheme.

- Reported financial loss: INR 3300000
- Payment method: `bank_transfer`
