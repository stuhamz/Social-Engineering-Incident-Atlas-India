## Neutral case summary

An Indian Army jawan posted in Jaisalmer was arrested after investigators said he had been honey-trapped by a social-media persona using the name Anika Chopra. The account presented itself as a young woman linked to Jammu and used intimate messages to cultivate the soldier. Police said the soldier then shared confidential information, including photographs of weapons and Army-company locations, through social media and WhatsApp. Investigators attributed the persona to Pakistani intelligence activity. For Atlas purposes, the social-engineering operator is the unresolved persona/handler rather than the soldier, who was the target of the honeytrap even though he was also the person arrested under espionage-related law.
