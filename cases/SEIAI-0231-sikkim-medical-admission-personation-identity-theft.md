## Neutral case summary

A Sikkim High Court anticipatory-bail order concerns allegations that medical aspirants and their parents were deceived through promises of admission to Sikkim Manipal Institute of Medical Sciences. FIR No. 0004 dated 16 March 2019 alleged cheating by personation, forgery, criminal conspiracy, identity theft and cheating by personation using computer resources. The State told the court that substantial case-diary material directly implicated the applicant, while the defence denied involvement and noted that he was not named in the FIR. The court rejected anticipatory bail. Atlas uses the order to establish the admission-personation social-engineering pattern but does not infer a payment amount, first-contact channel or final guilt.
